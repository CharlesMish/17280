## Truthful public claims and nonclaims

**May be claimed:** unsigned wearable two-hand skeleton wristwatch; 2.4 Hz / 17,280 vph as a modelled rate; approximately 31.54 × 29.55 mm with 34.17 mm lug-to-lug and a ~10.06 mm sapphire-to-sapphire package; front and rear planar sapphire assemblies, intentionally not domed; four case-grown horn lugs with real coaxial spring bars, 18 mm charcoal FKM strap heads and a buckle; explicit wheel/pinion axes, tooth counts, module, signed ratios, separate Z planes, jewels, bridges, barrel, balance, hairspring and lever escapement; a certified two-hand motion works; a deterministic presentation-only axial explosion that returns exactly to assembled at `explode=0`.

**Must not be claimed:** production manufacturability or tolerance/shock certification; water resistance; a verified power reserve; functional winding, setting or keyless works; a seconds display; 4 Hz or 28,800 vph operation; a verified marketing jewel count; a model or maison name.

**Three conditions on the above.**

1. The 2.4 Hz claim is not currently credible from the artefact itself. Per N01 the only view of the rear identity reads "24 Hz". No public statement of the rate should ship until action 1 closes, because the images presently contradict the specification card.
2. If the two gear penetrations ship unrepaired, the plan's own remedy applies and should be honoured literally: a public `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`, and the work described as a mechanically informed interactive visualization rather than an unqualified physically exact movement.
