## Cross-view contradictions and unknowns

- Hands are clearly present on the front and as a lifted plane in V08 (V01–V03, V07, V08), yet V06 engraves “NO HANDS.” That is a copy/model contradiction, not an angle omission.
- Crystal exists as a detached plate in V08/V09 but does not produce an assembled glass read in V01, V02, or V07.
- Case color temperature shifts with lighting: dark-dominant in V01, silver-band dominant in V02/V05; identity holds, finish does not.
- Four gray flange dots (V01, V03, V05) could be indices or un-detailed fasteners; whole-object views do not resolve the intent.
- V09 omits the large gold wheel that dominates V01–V06; absence from that explosion is not proof the part is missing from the build.
- Visual overlaps among hands, escape wheel, and gold wheel in V03/V04 are not treated as collisions.
- Strap length, buckle, and far-side horn are never shown; wearability remains under-evidenced rather than disproven.
- Crown face mark (V10) is too faint to identify as a brand, function, or tool cue.

## Verdict

`NEEDS-ANOTHER-PASS`. The silhouette and open-movement contrast are already a product, but time-read, wearable junctions, assembled sapphire, and metal lighting still fail a normal-size visual release.
