> **Do not distribute the existing `release/` ZIPs.** They predate the current source and accepted post-5D repairs.

## 1. Disposition

The reviews do not support a design reset.

They support a bounded closeout with two parallel lanes:

1. **Mechanical truth:** close the two remaining exact rendered gear penetrations and rerun the complete train/mechanical matrix.
2. **Release communication:** prove, and only where necessary refine, the rear technical copy, time read, wearable/strap read, planar sapphire, metal finishing, and authored case silhouette in a new presentation-only release annex.

After both lanes pass, freeze one new release-candidate authority, build the public shell, and generate deterministic packages from the exact tested bytes.

The source watch is materially more resolved than the blind images communicated. That is good news, but source existence does not erase a perceptual failure. Conversely, a screenshot inference does not authorize a geometry change.

- the tensioned-cushion silhouette and authored asymmetry;
- the complete package, case, lug, strap, sapphire, movement, bridge, bearing, and readout geometry;
- all certified axes, Z planes, tooth counts, modules, pitch/root/tip radii, ratios, directions, and motion ownership, except the narrowly bounded working-profile/phase repairs described in Lane A;
- the lever escapement, pallet contact law, balance, Phase 4B motion works, and two-hand 2.4 Hz / 17,280-vph authority;
- the unsigned face and exact rear copy `2.4 Hz · 17 280 · TWO HANDS`;
- the rear host `ext:caseback`, the unmarked `ext:caseback-step`, and the crown-cap three-fold emblem;
- planar front/rear sapphire boundaries and the accepted one-optical-body-per-side ownership;

This roadmap authorizes no V2 sculpture by itself.

## 5. Execution roadmap

Lanes A and B may run in parallel. Neither is allowed to declare a release candidate until both close.

### Gate 0 — Reproduce the current authority before edits

Before either lane changes product or presentation code:

1. hash every current `src/` file and release-relevant root config;
2. reproduce `npm run build` and the current accepted runtime/package reports;
3. reproduce the two known failing gear-pair witnesses;
4. capture the current rear identity, whole-watch front, strap, sapphire, finish macro, and E1 states as matched baselines;
5. record the one known allowlisted `THREE.BufferGeometry.computeTangents()` diagnostic exactly; any different browser/page/request error fails closed;
6. preserve the current package top/bottom and all accepted authority hashes.

If the current source does not reproduce its accepted state, stop with a baseline mismatch rather than rebasing silently.

### Lane A — Mechanical truth closeout

#### A1. Barrel 80T / center pinion 12T

Repair only the working mesh of `barrel:wheel` versus `center:pinion`.

Preflight the exact actual rendered repeating-cycle interference, then use the smallest pair-specific working-profile and local clocking correction that preserves:

- both axes and center distance;
- 80/12 teeth, module, pitch/root/tip radii, world-Z slabs, ratio, direction, rate, and rigid compound ownership;
- barrel drum/arbor and Phase 4B center output authority;
- all neighboring train and support clearances.

The successful center/third and third/fourth pair-specific involute/prismatic repairs are precedents, not dimensions to copy blindly.

#### A2. Fourth wheel 56T / escape pinion 7T

Repair only the working mesh of `fourth:wheel` versus `escape:pinion`.

This pair has higher scope risk because the pinion shares the certified escape arbor. Preserve exactly:

- fourth and escape axes, teeth, module, pitch/root/tip radii, Z, ratio, rate, and compound rigidity;
- the 15-club escape-wheel profile, escape wheel functional phase, pallet faces, lock/drop/impulse traces, and complete beat law;
- all fourth/escape bearings, staffs, bridge supports, and package authority.

Do not rotate or reprofile the escape clubs to solve a pinion mesh.

#### Lane-A acceptance

For each repaired pair:

- actual world-transformed rendered geometry, not pitch circles or proxies;
- at least 8,193 states across the full repeating tooth cycle, plus local refinement at the minimum;
- zero unintended intersections and a positive, numerically resolved minimum clearance;
- unchanged legacy world-Z intervals and frozen train authority;
- adjacent pair and foreign-solid screens after each repair.

Then run one consolidated matrix covering:

1. barrel 80 / center pinion 12;
2. center wheel 64 / third pinion 10;
3. third wheel 60 / fourth pinion 8;
4. fourth wheel 56 / escape pinion 7;
5. escape wheel/pallet intended contacts under their separate signed-gap law;
6. every certified wheel/arbor/staff/support relationship.

If either pair cannot be made exact without moving an axis, changing a frozen radius/Z/ratio, or altering the escape-club profile, stop for a new bounded architectural decision.

### Lane B — Release Presentation Annex R1

R1 is additive evidence and presentation behavior. It does not rewrite historical 5D-C authority unless the owner later promotes it.

#### B1. Rear-copy factual gate

Use the exact copy and existing `ext:caseback` host.

1. Reproduce the current final rear view and a close crop.
2. Inspect the actual identity texture, glyph raster, mip/filter path, UV mapping, bump, and roughness contribution.
3. Determine why the period in `2.4` disappears at the intended release camera.
4. Apply the smallest legal render-only refinement if needed: glyph edge/raster quality, SDF/distance shaping, texture filtering/resolution, path placement, or a bounded rear camera refinement.

Acceptance:

- an independent reader who has not seen the source transcribes exactly `2.4 Hz · 17 280 · TWO HANDS` from the delivered rear frame;
- a distinct rendered mark exists between `2` and `4` at delivered resolution;
- same host, exact copy, same-steel roughness + recessed bump only;
- no albedo/paint/overlay/x-ray/geometry/bounds/finish-family change;
- `ext:caseback-step` remains unmarked.

If this cannot close within the authorized map/camera treatment, stop rather than change the wording or host.

#### B2. Neutral whole-watch/front proof

Add one named long-working-distance, narrow-FOV front elevation outside frozen engineering camera tables. Preserve the whole cushion, complete crown, visible lug terminals, and enough strap context.

At a 320 px-wide copy of the delivered frame, require:

- both hands independently recognizable as 10:10;
- the 12 marker distinguishable from the other cardinals;
- the complete case/aperture relationship readable without obvious perspective distortion;
- gold barrel primary, balance secondary, blue hands primary blue.

If camera and camera-specific neutral lighting cannot close hand readability, stop for a separate readout-authority decision. Do not silently add ticks, lume, a seconds hand, or recolor the movement.

#### B3. Wearable proof

Create at least one product frame that includes:

- the real horn/lug gap and spring-bar/head relationship;
- enough free FKM to read as a strap rather than a plinth;
- at least one keeper and the buckle in either the same frame or a matched companion frame.

Separate charcoal FKM from the showcase background with neutral value lighting or a bounded background/profile variant. Do not recolor the strap or alter its geometry. A numeric luminance threshold may be recorded as supporting evidence, but blind category recognition is the primary gate.

#### B4. Sapphire proof and diagnostic

Produce matched assembled frontal and oblique frames from the current one-optical-body-per-side topology.

Require:

- movement remains legible frontally;
- the planar front and rear sapphire each read as transparent material at a useful oblique;
- no double-volume ghost, gray lid, cyan sheet, or false internal Fresnel boundary.

If the existing material still fails, perform a read-only flat-ID/normal/owner/material-path diagnostic first. Do not assume `FrontSide` or `depthWrite:false` is the cause, and do not alter a boundary vertex. Any material-flag or optical-value change requires a new, narrow render-only authorization.

#### B5. Metal/finish proof

At an unchanged macro and whole-watch camera, compare the frozen presentation baseline with one separately named neutral raking/specular profile or structured neutral environment.

The value lift should come from controlled specular geometry, not a broad theatrical wash or recoloring. Require the existing perlage, côtes, anglage, case layering, and wheel depth to become identifiable without:

- crushed blacks or blown steel edges;
- resurrected roughness moiré/banding;
- a second warm family;
- flattening the gold barrel/balance hierarchy.

The accepted 5D-C profiles remain callable and unchanged.

#### B6. E1 communication

Keep the current explosion. Add only release-shell communication:

- an obvious labeled `Assembled ↔ Exploded` control;
- smooth deterministic interpolation and reduced-motion behavior;
- an optional restrained eight-layer list/legend outside the watch;
- one final exploded hero and progression strip from the release candidate.

Do not begin the deeper movement-only explosion for v1.

#### Lane-B acceptance suite

The release annex should contain, at minimum:

1. whole-watch hero;
2. long-working-distance front elevation;
3. front three-quarter;
4. strap/wearability proof;
5. balance/finish macro;
6. sapphire oblique;
7. rear exhibition with exact readable identity;
8. E1 assembled, 25/50/75/100%, and exploded hero.

Every matched comparison must use documented camera, pose, readout, exposure, and light values. Presentation changes are never evidence of mechanical clearance.

### Gate C — Freeze one release-candidate authority

After A and B pass:

1. produce a new immutable manifest for the exact current source-file set;
2. serialize exact executable runtime/package authority;
3. rerun build/typecheck, browser runtime, going train, Phase 4B, escapement, bearings/supports, package, finish, identity, sapphire, and E1 regressions;
4. preserve all historical reports and cite their relationship to the new RC;
5. record the exact allowed source diff and all report/image hashes;
6. name the result `RC1` only if every gate passes.

The old Phase-5D-C manifest must not be relabeled as current. Count-only sampling normalization must be explicit; physical arrays remain exact.

### Lane D — Public shell and runtime quality

Without changing the watch:

- add semantic title/description and an accessible canvas label;
- expose assembled/exploded, pause/resume, reset-view, and camera-help controls;
- provide visible keyboard focus, 44 px touch targets, and `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior;
- include a static poster, `noscript` content, and WebGL-unavailable fallback;
- retain the current zero-external-request property unless separately authorized;
- record startup, warm frame-time, draw-call/triangle/texture counts, resize/orbit/zoom behavior, ten explode cycles, and context-loss behavior;
- smoke-test Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit/Safari where available, plus desktop and mobile viewport/DPR cases;
- allowlist only the exact known tangent warning fingerprint; any new console/page/request failure blocks release.

Do not optimize or split the bundle during RC unless measurement shows a real release failure. Capture-dependent `preserveDrawingBuffer` changes are likewise a separate decision.

### Lane E — Licensing, deterministic packaging, and deployment

Before public distribution:

1. decide and document project/source/design licensing;
2. include the Three.js MIT notice and a dependency/SBOM inventory;
3. record a release-time dependency vulnerability audit without opportunistically upgrading frozen dependencies;
4. pin/document the supported Node range required by the locked Vite version;
5. create three versioned artifacts:
   - tested static-site ZIP;
   - source-handoff ZIP with current verifier and build instructions;
   - optional selected-evidence ZIP;
6. write an internal release manifest with source/runtime/dist/evidence hashes, toolchain, supported browsers, and truthful limitations;
7. normalize archive ordering, timestamps, permissions, and metadata; package twice and require identical SHA-256;
8. extract each ZIP to a fresh temporary directory, reject unsafe paths, verify every hash, rebuild the source handoff, and smoke-test the static artifact;
9. test the same static bytes at `/` and `/watch/`;
10. deploy the exact tested bytes without a host-side rebuild;
11. configure correct MIME/compression/cache/security headers and retain the previous verified artifact for rollback;
12. publish `SHA256SUMS.txt`, release notes, browser/performance/accessibility results, and deployment/rollback instructions.

## 6. Explicit V2/deferred work

Do not let these delay the first truthful release:

- visible cut crown fluting or revised crown grip sculpture;
- chamfered/faceted jewel bodies and deeper bridge sculpture;
- movement-only rigid-compound explosion;
- functional winding/setting/keyless works;
- model or maison identity;
- a 60-tick/lume/seconds redesign;
- domed sapphire or global package rescale;
- decorative sapphire graphics;
- broad material-family redesign;
- wholesale spec/config refactoring.

## 7. Rejected directions

Do not:

- reinterpret the existing strap/lugs as a display stand;
- add a seconds hand, 4 Hz rate, or 28,800-vph claim;
- change the exact rear wording;
- add a full dial merely because the current proof frame is dark;
- move Z planes or axes to make a screenshot clearer;
- modify sapphire geometry to make it more obvious;
- advertise the crown as visibly fluted while its current relief remains buried;
- claim power reserve, water resistance, production tolerances, shock performance, functional keyless works, or a verified marketing jewel count;
- regenerate the watch around a new global scale or one-config refactor before release.

## 8. Stop conditions

Stop and request a new bounded decision if:

- Gate 0 cannot reproduce current authority;
- a gear mesh cannot close without changing frozen axes/radii/Z/ratios or the escape-club/contact law;
- exact rear-copy legibility cannot close within same-host render-map/camera authority;
- hand/12 readability cannot close through the presentation annex and would require readout redesign;
- sapphire proof requires boundary geometry or optical-ownership change;
- a new unintended collision, package drift, browser error, or identity/finish regression appears;
- deterministic archives cannot reproduce byte-identically or extracted artifacts fail their smoke tests.

## 9. Truthful release language

After all mechanical gates pass, the project may be described as an unsigned, mechanically informed two-hand skeleton wristwatch visualization with:

- 2.4 Hz / 17,280-vph modeled rate;
- explicit train ratios and Z-stacked wheel/pinion ownership;
- modeled lever escapement, balance/hairspring, jewels, bridges, front/rear planar sapphire, horn lugs, spring bars, and charcoal FKM strap;
- approximate exterior dimensions 31.54 × 29.55 mm, 34.17 mm lug-to-lug, 32.87 mm including crown;
- approximately 9.34 mm metal-stack thickness and 10.0576 mm full sapphire/package Z span;
- deterministic presentation-only axial explosion with exact assembled restoration.

It must not be described as production-tolerance qualified, water resistant, functionally windable/settable, seconds-displaying, power-reserve certified, or jewel-count certified.

If either gear penetration is deliberately left open, publish both exact findings in `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` and retain the narrower phrase **mechanically informed interactive visualization**. Do not claim a physically exact train.

## 10. Definition of done

Closeout is complete only when:

- all four going-train mesh pairs have named actual-geometry reports with zero unintended penetration;
- the complete mechanical/package/escapement/Phase-4B regression passes from one frozen RC source;
- the rear line is independently transcribed exactly from the delivered frame;
- 10:10 and the 12 hierarchy read at 320 px without new dial furniture;
- one delivered frame unmistakably reads as a strapped wearable and includes real strap hardware;
- planar sapphire and existing finish families read credibly in matched annex proofs;
- historical 5D-C and E1 authority remain intact;
- the public shell is keyboard/touch/reduced-motion/fallback capable;
- static and source ZIPs reproduce, verify, extract, rebuild/smoke, and hash identically;
- the hosted bytes are exactly the tested bytes;
- current manifests, checksums, notices, known nonclaims, and rollback instructions ship with the release.
